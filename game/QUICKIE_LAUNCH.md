# Kiwi As Quickie — launch content

The free Quickie is now deliberately limited to **10 active, editorially verified questions**. The engine also refuses to serve Quickie rows whose `last_verified` is null.

## Product purpose

The Quickie is a complete mini-game, not a crippled demo: one intro, 10 shuffled single-choice challenges, scoring, winner/solo ending, rematch, then a clear host CTA to the NZ$9.99 Full Showdown.

## Curated mix

- Places — Wellington / capital
- Nature — Aoraki / Mount Cook
- Icons — $1 coin / kiwi
- People — Helen Clark / elected PM wording
- Wildlife — kiwi
- Wildlife — kākāpō
- Wildlife — tuatara
- Kai / te ao Māori — hāngī
- Kiwi life — jandals
- History — women's suffrage 1893

All 10 rows carry an authoritative source and `last_verified = 2026-08-13`.

## Editorial rule

Do not expand the Quickie by copying arbitrary development questions into it. Any future Quickie question must be checked for factual accuracy, ambiguity, spelling/diacritics and cultural framing before `last_verified` is set.
